---
name: Screenplay evidence labels
description: Rules for distinguishing direct screenplay facts from mappings and heuristic source candidates
---
Only an exact normalized match in an actual screenplay content block may be labeled an exact textual mention. Scene mappings and owner-presence derivations are navigation/continuity data, not proof.

**Why:** A generic object noun can be a homonym, and AI or owner-derived scene mappings can include places where the object is not named.

**How to apply:** Keep screenplay sources separate from mapped scenes. Show Scene and Beat plus the quoted block. Label object-noun hits as “Possible match,” exclude headings and character cues, and state plainly when no direct mention exists.
