# Glass Hours — beats and screenplay

Short Film · Three-Act Turns · 16 scenes · ~15 min · original sample film for Kinolot (approved 2 October 2026).

Each scene runs about 55–60 seconds, roughly one screenplay page. All names, the hotel group (Ljusa Hotels) and
the businesses are invented.

## Changes from the approved spine

- **Scene 8 re-charted (peaks must alternate).** It was Trust +2 → Betrayal −2, so it closed negative like the
  Act I climax (scene 4, −2). It now opens at **−2**, when she learns Luca promised the buyer a chandelier behind
  her back, and closes at **+2**, when she takes the chandelier on, on her own terms, with Samir. Its axis is
  *Control ↔ Helplessness*. The events are the same. The peaks now run 4 (−2), 8 (+2), 12 (−3), 15 (+3).
- **One value axis per scene.** Each scene names one axis, "Positive pole ↔ Negative pole", and opens and closes on
  it. The approved numbers are kept, and the approved words become points on the axis:
  1 *Self-reliance ↔ Isolation* (her pride is self-reliance) · 2 *Reconciliation ↔ Rift* (his hope is for
  reconciliation) · 3 and 6 *Acceptance ↔ Rejection* (curiosity, then foothold, are the first steps toward being
  accepted) · 4 and 11 *Freedom ↔ Entrapment* (defiance and hope are freedom; cornered and trapped are entrapment) ·
  5 *Resolve ↔ Doubt* · 7 *Trust ↔ Mistrust* (guarded) · 9 *Mastery ↔ Doubt* (craft) · 10 *Pride ↔ Humiliation*
  (the wound) · 12 *Creation ↔ Ruin* (craft) · 13 *Understanding ↔ Estrangement* · 14 *Purpose ↔ Resignation* ·
  15 *Legacy ↔ Ruin* · 16 *Continuity ↔ Rupture* (uncertainty).
- Nothing else changed: same events, order, drivers and charges. The beats add only small props and people inside
  the approved events: the furnace mason Oreste (scene 4), a pencil line on the gas dial, the family's gold-ruby
  glass, the surveyor's chalk, the hotel's steel frame, and the 31 December deadline.

## Scene 1 — The Last Gather  (Act I)

- **Slugline:** INT. FIORIN FURNACE · DAWN
- **Driver:** Graziella
- **Reactor:** the glass
- **Value at stake:** Self-reliance ↔ Isolation
- **Open → close:** Pride +1 → Isolation −1
- **Conflict source:** extra-personal
- **Conflict mode:** reflexive
- **Structural role:** Hook

**Desire:** to finish the bowl alone.
**Obstacle:** the glass, to fall before she can.

**Beats**
1. Drive — ritual: she takes a gather and opens it into a bowl at the master's bench, alone, counting in breaths.
   React — gravity: the bowl begins to lean, wanting a second iron on its base now.
2. Drive — improvise: she rests the pipe in the yoke, keeps it rolling with one palm, and reaches for the pontello.
   React — distance: the iron is an arm's length too far, and her shoulder clicks a warning.
3. Drive — summon: out of forty years' habit she calls her husband's name for the iron.
   React — silence: only the furnace answers, with the poured cup on the empty second bench.
4. (turn) Drive — force: she lunges and makes the weld herself.
   React — collapse: the stiffened bowl tears free and bursts on the floor; she sweeps it into the cullet and drinks
   the cold coffee meant for no one.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FIORIN FURNACE, MURANO — DAWN

Black. A roar. Then a mouth of fire, and out of it, on the end
of a blowpipe, a ball of molten glass — swinging, turning,
never still.

GRAZIELLA FIORIN (72), leather apron over a man's cardigan,
walks it to the bench. Her lips move.

GRAZIELLA
(under her breath)
Uno... due... tre... quattro.

She sits at the scagno — the master's bench, its iron arms
worn bright — and rolls the pipe along them. A pad of wet
newspaper in her palm cups the glass. It opens into a bowl.

Beside her, a second bench. Nobody on it. On its arm, an
espresso cup, poured. No steam.

[b2]
The bowl begins to lean toward the floor.

She rests the pipe in the yoke, keeps it rolling with the flat
of her left hand, and reaches with her right for the pontello
heating in the furnace mouth. An arm's length too far. She
stretches. Her shoulder clicks.

[b3]
GRAZIELLA
Bepi. Il pontello.

The furnace answers. Nobody else.

She looks at the second bench. At the cup.

[b4]
She lunges, gets the iron, swings it to the bowl's base — but
the glass has stiffened. The weld won't take.

The bowl tears off the pipe and bursts on the brick. A small,
bright sound.

She sweeps the pieces into the cullet bin. One pass. Two.

Then she takes the cup from the empty bench and drinks the
cold espresso in one swallow.
```

## Scene 2 — The Offer  (Act I)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE OFFICE · DAY
- **Driver:** Luca
- **Reactor:** Graziella
- **Value at stake:** Reconciliation ↔ Rift
- **Open → close:** Hope +1 → Rift −2
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** indirect

**Desire:** to get her to sign the hotel's offer.
**Obstacle:** Graziella, to send him home without hearing it.

**Beats**
1. Drive — gift: Luca arrives from Milan and sets a panettone on her ledger.
   React — deflect: she moves it off the books; she touches his wet cuff, but only once.
2. Drive — arithmetic: he lays November's gas bill beside the €2.1m offer.
   React — mock: she laughs off Swedes who want a fire.
3. Drive — evidence: he names the broken bowl he saw in the cullet bin.
   React — banish: she moves him out of the draught and warns his coat off the furnace.
4. (turn) Drive — ultimatum: he uncaps a pen on the contract and names Friday.
   React — dismissal: she caps it, slides the folder back, and tells him he always knew the trains by heart. He
   leaves the folder anyway.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE OFFICE — DAY

A cupboard of a room off the furnace floor. Ledgers, a gas bill
under a glass paperweight, a bank calendar. Through the
partition window, the furnace glows.

Graziella at the desk, reading glasses on.

LUCA FIORIN (34), good navy coat, trouser hems dark with
seawater, sets a tall ribboned box on her ledger.

LUCA
From Milan. The one you like.

GRAZIELLA
Off the books, please.

She moves the panettone to the floor. As he sits, she touches
his wet cuff. Once.

GRAZIELLA (CONT'D)
You walked through the acqua alta in those.

[b2]
He slides the gas bill out from under the paperweight.

LUCA
Eleven thousand. November.

He lays a folder beside it, open at one line.

LUCA (CONT'D)
Two million one hundred thousand. A hotel group. Swedish.

GRAZIELLA
What do Swedes want with fire? They have saunas.

[b3]
LUCA
There's a bowl in the cullet bin. This morning's.

GRAZIELLA
I've broken a thousand.

LUCA
You've never broken one in your life. Papà used to say —

GRAZIELLA
Not by the door. The draught.
(beat)
And keep that coat away from the furnace. You'll go back to
Milan smelling of it.

[b4]
He uncaps a pen and lays it across the folder.

LUCA
They want an answer by Friday. I told them you'd at least —

She caps the pen. Closes the folder. Slides both back to him.

GRAZIELLA
The four-ten gets you to Milan before dark.
(beat)
You always knew the trains by heart.

LUCA
Mamma, I'm sor —

He stands. Leaves the folder on the stool by the door. Goes.

She doesn't look at it.
```

## Scene 3 — The Boy at the Window  (Act I)

- **Slugline:** EXT. FONDAMENTA DEI VETRAI · DUSK
- **Driver:** Samir
- **Reactor:** Graziella
- **Value at stake:** Acceptance ↔ Rejection
- **Open → close:** Curiosity +1 → Rejection −1
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** asymmetric

**Desire:** to get closer to the work.
**Obstacle:** Graziella, to drive him off.

**Beats**
1. Drive — watch closer: Samir wipes his breath off the window and counts along with her.
   React — block: she turns her stool so that her back fills the pane.
2. Drive — mimic: at the next pane he turns his thermos in time with her pipe.
   React — confront: she comes out and asks who he is counting her tools for.
3. Drive — offer: he offers to carry in her wet sacks of sand.
   React — accuse: she names his masters for him: the Swedes, her son.
4. (turn) Drive — name the need: he tells her he saw the dawn bowl fall for lack of a second pair of hands.
   React — reject: she gives him the word "pontello" before she can stop herself, then sends him back to his boats
   and drops the shutter.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
EXT. FONDAMENTA DEI VETRAI — DUSK

Lamps coming on along the canal. A vaporetto shoulders past;
its wash slaps the steps.

SAMIR HADDAD (19), a deckhand's waterproof, rope-roughened
hands, stands at the furnace window. His breath fogs the glass.
He wipes it with his cuff and leans in.

Inside, Graziella turns a pipe. His lips move with her count.

She feels it. Without looking up, she turns her stool until
her back fills the window.

[b2]
Samir steps to the next pane. Lifts his thermos and turns it
slowly between his palms, in time with her.

The side door bangs open. Graziella, wiping her hands.

GRAZIELLA
Every evening. You're counting my tools for somebody?

[b3]
SAMIR
I finish at six. My boat is at twenty past.

He nods at the sacks of sand slumped by her door, dark with
wet.

SAMIR (CONT'D)
These are getting wet. I can carry them in —

GRAZIELLA
Who sent you? The Swedes? My son?

SAMIR
Nobody sends me.

[b4]
He keeps his eyes on hers.

SAMIR (CONT'D)
This morning, the first boat. I saw. You need somebody for the
small iron. For the bottom.

A long breath.

GRAZIELLA
Pontello.

SAMIR
(under his breath)
Pontello.

GRAZIELLA
Now go back to your boats.

She steps inside and hauls the shutter down over the window.
Iron on stone.

Samir stands in front of it. Then he walks to the landing, his
lips still moving.
```

## Scene 4 — The Crack  (Act I)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE · NIGHT
- **Driver:** Graziella
- **Reactor:** Oreste, the furnace mason (and the furnace)
- **Value at stake:** Freedom ↔ Entrapment
- **Open → close:** Defiance +1 → Cornered −2
- **Conflict source:** extra-personal
- **Conflict mode:** balanced
- **Structural role:** Inciting Incident · Act I climax

**Desire:** to keep the fire burning on her own terms.
**Obstacle:** Oreste, to make her face what the crown is telling her.

**Beats**
1. Drive — defy: to fill an order, she turns the gas past her husband's pencil line on the dial.
   React — rupture: the crown cracks like a shot and a seam of white light opens in the brick.
2. Drive — downplay: she calls the old mason about "a little noise".
   React — diagnose: Oreste taps along the crown and gets a dead note everywhere.
3. Drive — bargain: she asks for a patch, like the one in 1998.
   React — price: a new crown costs forty-eight thousand, more than she sold all last year.
4. (turn) Drive — retrench: she says she will work small, glasses and beads.
   React — sentence: Oreste turns her fire down himself (a month at low heat, then it falls) and asks who holds for
   her now. She lifts the weight off Luca's folder.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE — NIGHT

Thirty goblets cooling on a rack, an order for a restaurant
on Burano. Luca's folder on the stool by the door, a goblet
foot on it for a paperweight.

Graziella at the gas valve. On the dial, a faded pencil line
in a man's hand: 1150. She turns past it. The roar deepens.
She works faster.

A crack like a shot.

Across the furnace's domed crown, a seam of white light has
opened in the brick.

[b2]
LATER

ORESTE (78), the furnace mason, overcoat over pyjamas, taps a
steel rod along the crown. A dead note. Further along. Dead.

ORESTE
You said a little noise.

GRAZIELLA
It was little.

ORESTE
I built this crown with Bepi. Ninety-eight. I know how she
talks.

[b3]
GRAZIELLA
So patch her. Like ninety-eight.

ORESTE
In ninety-eight it was one crack. Now she's tired all through.
New crown, bricks from Germany, six weeks to dry.
(beat)
Forty-eight thousand.

GRAZIELLA
I sold forty-one last year.

ORESTE
I know what you sold. I drink with your accountant.

[b4]
GRAZIELLA
Then I work small. Glasses. Beads. Beads don't need a crown.

Oreste reaches past her and turns the valve down, well below
Bepi's line. The roar sinks to a mutter.

ORESTE
At this she holds a month. Christmas, maybe. Then she comes
down on whoever's under her.
(beat)
Who's holding for you now, Graziella?

She doesn't answer. He goes.

She crosses to the stool and lifts the goblet foot off Luca's
folder. She doesn't open it. She doesn't step away from it.
```

## Scene 5 — Terms  (Act II)

- **Slugline:** INT. HOTEL BAR, LIDO · DAY
- **Driver:** Luca
- **Reactor:** Annika
- **Value at stake:** Resolve ↔ Doubt
- **Open → close:** Doubt −1 → Resolve +1
- **Conflict source:** extra-personal
- **Conflict mode:** indirect

**Desire:** to sell the furnace without selling his mother.
**Obstacle:** Annika, to fix the price and the date on the hotel's terms.

**Beats**
1. Drive — hedge: Luca asks for time until Easter.
   React — reframe: Annika shows him the furnace as a dark "feature" behind glass in a lobby.
2. Drive — probe: he points out that it won't be lit.
   React — leverage: she knows about the cracked crown, which he didn't, and sets the 31st as the deadline.
3. Drive — defend: he insists his mother still works every dawn.
   React — sweeten: then the hotel wants something she made: a Fiorin chandelier over reception.
4. (turn) Drive — commit: without asking his mother, he writes the chandelier into the terms and initials it.
   React — seal: Annika fixes the 31st and books a surveyor for Thursday.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. HOTEL BAR, LIDO — DAY

Off-season. Dust sheets over half the armchairs. Beyond the
windows, bathing huts shuttered against a grey Adriatic.

ANNIKA LUND (41), cashmere and reading glasses, turns a tablet
toward Luca. His coffee is untouched.

LUCA
She needs until spring. Easter. Let her finish the season.

ANNIKA
I hear you. Look at this first.

On the tablet: the Fiorin furnace floor as a hotel lobby. The
furnace kept, dark, behind a wall of glass, lit from below
like a museum piece. A bar where the benches stand.

ANNIKA (CONT'D)
We keep the heart of it. A feature.

[b2]
LUCA
A feature. It won't be lit.

ANNIKA
It can't be lit, Luca. The crown is cracked.

He didn't know. It shows.

ANNIKA (CONT'D)
Murano is a small island. Her mason gave our surveyor a figure.
(gently)
Two-point-one stands if we sign by the thirty-first. After
that, we start again from the walls.

[b3]
LUCA
She still works. Every day. From dawn.

ANNIKA
Then I'd like something she made. Over reception. A
chandelier. Fiorin glass, under the Fiorin name.
(beat)
Guests love a story.

[b4]
Luca turns his cup a quarter turn on its saucer.

LUCA
She'll make it. Put it in the terms.

ANNIKA
Without asking her?

LUCA
She'll make it for strangers.
(beat)
She always has.

Annika writes on the printed terms and initials it. He
initials beside her: L.F.

ANNIKA
The thirty-first, then. I'll send someone to measure on
Thursday.
```

## Scene 6 — Cullet  (Act II)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE · NIGHT
- **Driver:** Samir
- **Reactor:** Graziella
- **Value at stake:** Acceptance ↔ Rejection
- **Open → close:** Rejection −1 → Foothold +1
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** asymmetric
- **Structural role:** Pinch point 1. The market has measured her furnace for its bar.

**Desire:** to be let in.
**Obstacle:** Graziella, to get rid of him for good.

**Beats**
1. Drive — gift: Samir brings a fish crate of glass he hooked out of the canal at the stops.
   React — dismiss: she throws back bottle glass that "doesn't marry" with hers.
2. Drive — inform: he tells her he ferried the surveyors who chalked her furnace this afternoon, and shows her where
   they said the bar goes.
   React — accuse: she scrubs at the chalk and calls him theirs.
3. Drive — offer: he asks to work nights for nothing.
   React — eject: she boots the crate off her threshold, and the glass skates across the stones.
4. (turn) Drive — reveal: he unwraps the one shard he kept apart, in the family's gold-ruby red.
   React — test: she knows it at once, and sets him a time: tomorrow at ten, in gloves he doesn't love.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE — NIGHT

Low fire. The seam in the crown glows like a held breath.

On the brick beside the furnace mouth, someone else's yellow
chalk: 2,40 × 1,80. An arrow. BAR? On the furnace door, a
printed sticker: RETAIN — FEATURE.

Graziella scrubs the chalk with a wet rag. It smears to a
yellow ghost and stays.

A knock. Samir in the doorway, a white fish crate in his arms,
heavy with glass: bottle green, brown, one cobalt blue. Still
dripping.

SAMIR
From the canal. I fish it at the stops, with the hook.

She picks out a green shard. Tosses it back.

GRAZIELLA
Bottle. It doesn't marry with mine. It cracks.

[b2]
He nods at the chalk.

SAMIR
I brought them. This afternoon, on the four-one. Two men and a
laser.
(points at the floor under her feet)
They said the bar goes there.

She scrubs harder. The ghost spreads.

GRAZIELLA
So you work for them.

SAMIR
I work for the boats.

[b3]
SAMIR (CONT'D)
After my last boat, until the first one, I can work here. For
nothing.

GRAZIELLA
Nobody works for nothing. One day they want something.

She shoves the crate back over the threshold with her boot. It
tips. Glass skates across the wet stones.

[b4]
Samir kneels and gathers it back, piece by piece. From inside
his jacket he takes a folded handkerchief. One shard, kept
apart. A deep red, struck with gold.

SAMIR
This one is different. Heavy.

She takes it to the furnace light. Turns it.

GRAZIELLA
(barely)
That's our red.

SAMIR
Cimitero stop. Under the landing.

She closes her hand on it. Four breaths.

GRAZIELLA
Tomorrow. Ten o'clock.
(beat)
Bring gloves you don't love.
```

## Scene 7 — First Burn  (Act II)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE · NIGHT
- **Driver:** Graziella
- **Reactor:** Samir
- **Value at stake:** Trust ↔ Mistrust
- **Open → close:** Guarded −1 → Trust +2
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** minimal

**Desire:** to break him before he breaks something of hers.
**Obstacle:** Samir, to hold on.

**Beats**
1. Drive — haze: she makes him turn an empty pipe for ten minutes without stopping.
   React — endure: his forearms shake; he repeats "don't stop" under his breath.
2. Drive — raise the heat: she swaps a live gather onto his pipe.
   React — comply: he keeps the gather round, repeating her words.
3. Drive — trap: she tells him to choke up the pipe, onto iron she knows is hot.
   React — hold: the iron burns his palm, and he keeps turning.
4. (turn) Drive — break her rule: she takes his hand, cools it and dresses it, and tells him about her husband's
   hands.
   React — yield: he lets her. She gives him the pontello for tomorrow.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE — NIGHT

Samir at the second bench in gloves worn through at the
fingers. An empty blowpipe across the iron arms.

GRAZIELLA
Turn it. Don't stop till I say.

He rolls it along the arms. She sits with her coffee and
watches the clock above the lehr. Ten minutes. His forearms
shake.

SAMIR
(under his breath)
Don't stop. Don't stop.

[b2]
She takes a gather from the furnace on a second pipe and
swaps it into his hands. Fire, a hand's breadth from his face.

GRAZIELLA
Now it's real. Turn. Never stop.

SAMIR
(under his breath)
Turn. Never stop.

The gather stays round. She circles him once.

[b3]
GRAZIELLA
Choke up. Hands closer to the glass.

He slides his hands down the pipe, toward the end where the
iron has drunk the heat. A hiss. Singed wool.

His jaw locks. He keeps turning. The gather stays round.

She lets him go four breaths. Five.

[b4]
She takes the pipe and sets the piece in the lehr. Then she
takes his wrist and pushes his hand into the quench bucket.
Holds it there.

He looks at her hands. Old burns, white, on both palms.

She wraps his hand in a clean rag, slowly.

GRAZIELLA
My husband's hands were like the bottom of a pan. He could
hold a cup straight from the machine.
(beat)
At the end he couldn't feel one at all.

She ties the rag off.

GRAZIELLA (CONT'D)
Forty-one years he held for me. He never let one drop.

She looks at him.

GRAZIELLA (CONT'D)
Tomorrow you hold the pontello.
```

## Scene 8 — The Promise  (Act II)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE · DAY
- **Driver:** Luca
- **Reactor:** Graziella
- **Value at stake:** Control ↔ Helplessness
- **Open → close:** −2 → +2 (re-charted, see "Changes from the approved spine")
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** balanced
- **Structural role:** Mid-Act Climax. She stops refusing and starts acting.

**Desire:** to get her to accept the promise he made for her.
**Obstacle:** Graziella, to refuse to be sold along with the building.

**Beats**
1. Drive — fait accompli: Luca hands her the hotel's chandelier specification, which reads "as agreed with
   L. Fiorin".
   React — read: she reads it twice and lays it on the marver, very flat.
2. Drive — flatter: her name will hang over a five-star door.
   React — expose: she taps his initials: her name, in his handwriting.
3. Drive — undermine: a chandelier 140 centimetres across, at low fire, alone, after a dropped bowl.
   React — counter-evidence: she shows him the second cup and the scorched gloves on the second bench.
4. (turn) Drive — dismiss: he says she never lets anyone near that bench, and stops just before "me".
   React — seize: she takes his pen, strikes out his name, writes hers and Samir's with her terms, and opens up the
   fire.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE — DAY

Grey daylight. On the marver, two espresso cups, both drained.
On the second bench, a pair of scorched gloves.

Luca in his coat. Graziella holds a printed sheet at arm's
length.

INSERT — THE SHEET: LJUSA HOTELS · MURANO HOUSE · RECEPTION
CHANDELIER, Ø 140 CM, FIORIN GLASS. INSTALLED BY 31.12. AS
AGREED WITH L. FIORIN. Initials: L.F.

She reads it twice. Lays it on the marver, very flat.

[b2]
LUCA
They want your name over their door. People will come to
Murano for it.

GRAZIELLA
My name.
(taps the initials)
In your hand.

[b3]
LUCA
A hundred and forty centimetres. At low fire, with that crown.
You dropped a bowl on your own.

GRAZIELLA
Who said on my own?

She lifts one of the two cups and turns it so he can see the
coffee ring inside. He looks at the gloves on the second
bench. At the scorch marks.

LUCA
You don't let anyone near that bench. You never once let —

He stops.

[b4]
She holds out her hand. He doesn't understand. She takes the
pen from his breast pocket.

One clean line through AS AGREED WITH L. FIORIN. Under it, in
careful schoolroom script:

INSERT — THE SHEET: MADE BY G. FIORIN AND S. HADDAD. NOTHING IS
SIGNED UNTIL IT HANGS.

She caps the pen, puts it back in his pocket, hands him the
sheet.

GRAZIELLA
Tell your Swedes they'll have their chandelier.

LUCA
Who is S. Haddad?

She turns to the furnace and opens the valve, up to Bepi's
line and no further.
```

## Scene 9 — Reticello  (Act II)

- **Slugline:** INT. FURNACE · NIGHTS
- **Driver:** Graziella
- **Reactor:** Samir
- **Value at stake:** Mastery ↔ Doubt
- **Open → close:** Doubt −1 → Craft +2
- **Conflict source:** personal
- **Conflict mode:** comic

**Desire:** to teach him the net pattern before the month runs out.
**Obstacle:** Samir, to learn it his own fast way.

**Beats**
1. Drive — demonstrate: she makes the first spiral cup and gives him the rule: every thread crosses another.
   React — rush: he over-twists his cup into a knot.
2. Drive — diagram: she chalks a net over the surveyor's yellow ghost.
   React — improvise: he splices two hot threads like a mooring line, and the cup folds in on itself.
3. Drive — slow him: four breaths before every touch, because glass forgets slowly.
   React — comply under protest: he repeats the rule and bargains with it.
4. (turn) Drive — withdraw: she goes and sits by the window and leaves the glass to him.
   React — achieve: the net forms, with a bead of his breath caught in every diamond.

**Screenplay**

```
[b1]
INT. FURNACE — NIGHT

Samir's right hand in a fat white bandage.

Graziella lays thin white canes side by side in a ribbed
mould, like matches in a box. She picks them up on a gather
and twists: a cup striped in a slow white spiral.

GRAZIELLA
Now one the other way. One inside the other. Every thread
crosses another.

Samir's cup twists the other way: fast, eager, three turns too
many. The threads bunch into a knot.

GRAZIELLA (CONT'D)
That's a rope.

SAMIR
I know ropes.

GRAZIELLA
I know you know ropes.

[b2]
ANOTHER NIGHT

The bandage smaller. Over the surveyor's yellow ghost,
Graziella chalks a net: lines crossing lines.

GRAZIELLA
Not one of them lies alone.

Samir nods hard. Then he tries his own way, pinching two hot
threads together quick, like a splice on a mooring line.
```
