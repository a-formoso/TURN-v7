The service

Creating an account is free and lets you look around. Creating stories and generating media use credits, because those features spend real compute.

Plans and credits

Producer adds the Stage (video). Without a plan, credits (from the free trial or a credit pack) cover writing on the default engines and draft images only.
  • New accounts get 50 free credits once, after confirming their email address — enough to try the Writers' Room on the lighter writing engine (a logline, a synopsis, the spine and its first scenes), plus draft images. Trial credits have no cash value.

  • Credit packs are separate, and any signed-in account can buy one: 250 credits for $10, 1,000 for $35 or 3,000 for $99. Pack credits last 12 months from purchase and are never reset when your plan renews. We use your plan's monthly credits first, then pack credits, the soonest to expire first. Credits we give you (for example after a problem) also last 12 months.

The one exception: if the provider never tells us the outcome within 30 days, we keep the credits set aside for it (email us and we'll look into it). If a render fails, is cancelled or never starts, its credits are returned in full, even if you closed the app before it finished.

For Motion Control and video edits the app shows what the render sets aside; you're charged by the length of the video it produces, never more than that.

Payments, plan changes and refunds

  • To switch plans, choose Change plan in your account menu. For now that means writing to kinolot-billing@infinitestudioai.com from your account email, and we move you over.

Your renewal date stays the same, and your next renewal gives the bigger plan's full allowance.

Your plan and credits keep working while it retries, but your monthly credits don't refill until a payment goes through. If every retry fails, the subscription ends and its monthly credits end with it; pack credits stay usable until they expire.

If we refund a payment in full, the unused credits it added are removed (for a plan payment, the ones it added for the current month), including credits set aside for a generation that is still running.
